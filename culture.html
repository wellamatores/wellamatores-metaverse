🌕 LEGEND OF THE MOONS: "TWO THAT ARE SEEN"

Clan oral tradition: "In the beginning were Three Suns and Four Moon-Sisters. But two sisters hid in the Dance of Shadows, and people see only two. When the Stone of Death falls, the sisters emerge from shadow to avert the disaster."

Scientific explanation (Le Bureau:Jackdaw): The system has four moons. Two on stable orbits — Le Bouclier and La Lance. Two others — L'Observateur and Le Vagabond — move on complex gravitational trajectories caused by interaction of three suns (trinary system).
