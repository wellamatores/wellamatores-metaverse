🐸 I · THE FIRST CROAK

B

efore the Three Suns ignited, before the Four Sisters began their dance — there was Silence. Deep. Wet. Saline. The silence of the primordial ocean.

In that silence the fish swam without words. The jellyfish drifted without song. The first lizards dreamed without names. Then One crawled out. From water onto land. Wet, round-eyed, restless.

She breathed air for the first time — it burned her lungs. And she croaked.

✦ KVA shattered the Silence ✦

From KVA came waves. From waves — vibration. From vibration — the first echo. And from that click, the Three Suns burst into flame.

The Frog looked at them. Then turned. She jumped back into the water. But KVA remained — hanging in the air, echoing in the stones, whispering in the roots.

"The Frog doesn't care."

LE BUREAU NOTE: Three interpretations — Urvids say joy, Frost say terror, Whisper claim absolute indifference. The First Croak has no single meaning. Yet it echoes for 370 million years.

🌀 IV · THE TWO SPIRITS (SYMBIONTS)

"The Frog left two gifts on land. They live within us still."

🔥 Little Warm One — lives in the chest. It fears, it rejoices, it warns of danger. Mammal-endosymbiont, emotional anchor.
❄️ Little Cold One — lives in the bones. Sees in darkness, knows when to strike or freeze. Reptile/amphibian core, instinct of survival.

The bond forms between ages 6 and 8. Lasts until death. Severance is lethal in 97% of cases. Other animals lost their spirits long ago. But the 36 clans remember.

👁️ VI · THE STRANGERS (LE BUREAU)

A thousand years after the Great Diverting, a star fell from the sky — but it was no star. A white vessel, silent and gleaming. Strangers emerged with strange faces and stranger tongue.

"We are Le Bureau des Colonies. We have come to observe."

The clans raised spears. But the strangers did not fight. They stood still. The shaman of Urvids walked forward and spoke: "You do not need our lands. You need our stories. Follow me. I will tell you of KVA."
