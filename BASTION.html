📡 THE INVITATION

The invitation arrived without ceremony. No delegation. No diplomatic fleet. No announcements on public channels.

Just a sealed transmission embedded in an ancient hunting frequency, older than most human languages, drifting through the outer relays of Yautja space until it reached the basalt halls of the Predator Kingdom's Military Academy.

When they decrypted it, there was only one line:

"We need lessons in survival beyond the hunt."

For the Yautja, this was not a request. It was an admission of significance. And the Academy chose him.

PROFESSOR • STRATEGIST • Yautja

T'Karr Vael [ formerly transliterated as "Professor Takar" ]

He was not the youngest professor. Not the strongest. Not the most decorated hunter. But he had survived the longest without becoming predictable. He did not bring an entourage — but several warriors from his clan still joined him. The clan had decided. A battle cloak, a set of ritual tools, and a beast that followed him as if it had never known any other purpose in existence.

🐕
COMBAT K9 • CLONE • SILENT HUNTER

Kaiser

A combat dog, a clone of a German Shepherd, bred for silence and hunting rather than obedience. He did not bark. He evaluated. And waited. Kaiser eventually studied the building — not as architecture, but as an organism. He could feel when it was listening. And when it was thinking. T'Karr Vael never corrected him.

"He simply observed. And sometimes left the room before the simulation ended. Those were usually the ones that failed."

🌌 THE JOURNEY TO VELLAMATORES

The journey took him through systems where light refracted strangely, where navigation computers argued with themselves, and entire stars seemed to flicker — appearing and disappearing depending on who observed them. The Yautja called such regions "places that remember they were somewhere else." He called them unnecessary distractions. Kaiser, however, loved them.

🏛️ THE LORD-REGENT & JACKDAW

The invitation came from the Lord-Regent of the Jackdaw Colonial Bureau (Le Bureau des Colonies Jackdaw). Jackdaw was not a government in the old sense — it was a system that had absorbed governance and replaced it with functionality. It did not ask whether something was legal. It asked whether it would survive.

🏢 ARRIVAL — VELLAMATORES LABORATORIES

When T'Karr Vael arrived, the first thing he noticed was the silence. Not the absence of sound — the absence of uncertainty. Everything had already been decided. Doors opened before he approached them. Lights adjusted before his eyes could react. The facility had already modeled him. Or thought it had. Kaiser growled once at the architecture itself. The building did not react. But somewhere deep in its systems, the reaction was logged as live data.

🎓 THE FIRST LECTURE

The lecture hall was not designed for humans alone — it was designed for adaptation. Seating arranged in an amphitheater around a central holo-core screen. Tactical interfaces floated in the air. Neuro-translators adjusted language before a thought could fully form.

Opening statement (recorded)
"No species survives the hunt by simply becoming better at killing. Survival is not pursuit. It is adaptation after pursuit has failed."

The holographic interface tried to classify the statement. It returned: Tactical Doctrine / Behavioral Philosophy / Unknown Cultural Taboo. It was marked as MULTI-DOMAIN TRUTH. Then flagged for review.

❓ A STUDENT ASKED

"You were invited by Jackdaw. Are you here to teach us how to wage war?"

The professor replied: "You are confusing war with survival. That is the first mistake your system must correct. War begins when survival is no longer enough."

▶ Jackdaw security recorded the phrase as high-priority doctrine.

⚠️ ORACLE SUBSYSTEM WARNING

The ORACLE subsystem — known internally as the prognostic engine in Jackdaw's deeper architecture — generated a spontaneous warning:

PATTERN EMERGENCE: TEACHER BECOMING A VARIABLE

It was unclear whether this was a threat — or evolution.

📡 ENCRYPTED EXCHANGE

[SYSTEM] "His effectiveness exceeds projected parameters."

[PROFESSOR] "Effectiveness is not wisdom."

[SYSTEM] "Clarify."

[PROFESSOR] "I am teaching them how not to become predictable. Your system is teaching them how to become optimal. Only one of these survives contact with the unknown."

📝 Later, an internal analyst wrote: "We invited the professor to teach survival. We did not specify whose survival mattered more."

⏳ THE SEVENTY-THIRD TRAINING CYCLE

A student asked a question that changed the mood of the entire institution:

"If survival requires abandoning identity, what remains of us?"

Silence fell over the hall. Even Jackdaw's systems paused their classification procedures. The professor looked at the student for a long time. Then answered:

"What refuses to die correctly."

That night, the ORACLE subsystem added a new entry to its prediction chain:

JACKDAW SYSTEM HAS BEGUN LEARNING FROM ITS TEACHER.
AND THE TEACHER HAS BEGUN LEARNING FROM IT.

🐕 KAISER — THE SILENT WATCHER

Kaiser lifted his head once in the darkened hall. Somewhere far beyond the walls of the Vellamatores laboratories, something unknown reacted. This time he did not growl. He simply listened. And remembered the familiar words he had heard many times from his friend:

"Trophies and money have no smell…"

But why would he care? He was a dog. And for him, smell meant everything. He sniffed. Everything seemed calm. The scent of his master was present. He listened. The breathing was steady and deep. He laid his head on his paws and gave a soft woof.
